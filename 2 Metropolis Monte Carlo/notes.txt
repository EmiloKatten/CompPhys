What is the difference between Week1 Markow chains and Week2 Metropolis Monte carlo Markow chains

In week 1:  Tij' was a matrix containing the probabilites of transitioning from i to j, which determined where the states moved.

In week 2:  Tij is a proposal distribution, with probabilites of the transition being proposed. 
            Afterwards, we accept that transition with an acceptence probability Aij.


Tij' = Tij*Aij  for i != j
Tij' = 1 - sum_{i!=j} Tij*Aij  for i = j

So the diagonal is the probability of staying.
